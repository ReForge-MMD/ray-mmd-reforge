Exclusive Features

The definitive MMD rendering experience, re-architected from the ground up.

Next-Gen PCSS & Contact Shadows

True physical contact-hardening shadows based on solar angular diameter (0.53°), 4-cascade cross-blending, and sub-millimeter Screen-Space Contact Shadows (SSCS) that eliminate Peter-Panning.

Vectorized SIMD SSR & SSGI 3.0

Optimized 4-sample SIMD batching for raymarching without warp divergence, progressive multi-bounce indirect lighting, slope-adaptive tolerance, and dedicated reflect-visibility maps.

Hybrid GTAO / HBSSDO

Horizon-based ambient occlusion and directional occlusion with occlusion-aware bent normals for believable indirect shading without darkening skin contours.

AgX, Punchy & Khronos PBR Neutral

Official 6th-order Blender 4.0 AgX color science, high-contrast AgX Punchy, and official Khronos PBR Neutral tonemapping for highlight-preserving, unclipped color fidelity.

5x5 Calibrated Tonal Skin & UE5 SSS

Calibrated 5x5 Fitzpatrick tonal skin suite for PBR and Toon shading, dual-lobe microfacet specular, Henyey-Greenstein backlit tissue glow, and camera-invariant UV-space diffusion.

Marschner Hair & Hashed Alpha

Dual-highlight tangent-shifted anisotropic specular (R + TRT), energy-preserving GGX, and Wyman & McGuire band-limited hashed alpha cutout without fringe crawling grain.

Corneal Dome Eyes & Iris Parallax

Automatic convex normal inversion (CONVEX_NORMAL), Snell's law refraction parallax for deep pupil immersion, analytical Jorge Jimenez iris caustics, and dedicated unshadowed highlights.

10-Domain Modular Architecture

Fully reorganized 10-domain shader pipeline with zero broken includes, alongside decoupled PMX controllers (LightingController, CameraController, BloomController, DebugController).

Temporal Anti-Aliasing (TAA)

5-tap Catmull-Rom bicubic history reconstruction, Karis luma weighting, variance clipping, and depth-validated history for a stable, flicker-free image.

FidelityFX CAS & Cinematic Bokeh

AMD FidelityFX Contrast-Adaptive Sharpening, hexagonal bokeh DOF with physical iris blade controls, anti-firefly Karis bloom, and spectral film halation.

Glass & Chromatic Dispersion

Physical RGB chromatic dispersion refraction, tinted glass presets inheriting MMD diffuse color, and forced-transparency material presets.

Foliage Translucency & Cloth

Energy-conserving Steve McAuley two-sided wrapped transmission for leaves and paper, Charlie sheen cloth BRDF, and vertex wind animation engine.
