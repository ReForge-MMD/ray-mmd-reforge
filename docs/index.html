Feature index

Rendering systems

Twelve systems replace the stock MMD lighting path, from shadow contact to the final grade.

  1. 01

    Contact-hardening shadows

    PCSS models the 0.53° solar disk across four cascades, with screen-space contact shadows for the last few centimetres.

  2. 02

    Screen-space GI and reflections

    SSGI and SSPT gather indirect bounces. SSR adds roughness-aware reflections with visibility maps that keep edges stable.

  3. 03

    Horizon-based occlusion

    GTAO and hybrid HBAO/SSDO use occlusion-aware bent normals, so contact shading stays readable on faces and cloth.

  4. 04

    AgX and Khronos tonemapping

    Blender 4.0 AgX, AgX Punchy, and Khronos PBR Neutral, with highlight roll-off that holds color instead of clipping to white.

  5. 05

    Calibrated skin and UE5 SSS

    A calibrated skin suite with dual-lobe specular, wrapped diffuse, backlit transmission, and UV-space diffusion.

  6. 06

    Hair and hashed alpha

    Marschner R and TRT highlights, energy-preserving GGX, and band-limited hashed alpha without fringe crawl.

  7. 07

    Eyes and iris parallax

    Convex corneal normals, Snell refraction parallax, and an analytical iris caustic for close-up shots.

  8. 08

    Ten-domain shader pipeline

    The Shader tree is split into ten domains, with dedicated PMX controllers for lighting, camera, bloom, and debug.

  9. 09

    Temporal anti-aliasing

    Catmull-Rom history, Karis luma weighting, variance clipping, and depth-validated reprojection.

  10. 10

    CAS, bokeh, and halation

    FidelityFX CAS, hexagonal bokeh with iris blades, Karis bloom, and spectral film halation.

  11. 11

    Glass and dispersion

    Physical RGB dispersion, tinted glass presets, and forced-transparency materials for windows and water.

  12. 12

    Foliage and cloth

    Two-sided wrapped transmission for leaves and paper, Charlie sheen for cloth, and vertex wind animation.
